2Material, motion, and Miso — the same flow, having more fun

Structure and layout are untouched; what changes is what's behind and between the cards. The flat paper ground becomes a lit one — soft ochre and terracotta light pooling off-canvas behind a faint paw-print weave — and chrome becomes material: the nav bar is a translucent blurred layer the content scrolls under, the primary action floats as a blurred capsule instead of sitting on a solid slab, and adding an expense is a sheet that springs up over the dimmed trip rather than a page swap. Miso carries the personality: he's the mark on the welcome screen answering the one question the app exists to answer, a real member chip in the avatar row, the live split calculator at the bottom of the add sheet, and the face of the completion screen — the app's emotional peak, now a full ochre field with a paw weave and the tally it earned. Each screen is shown on both grounds: warm paper keeps the existing identity and lets the light do the work; dark glass puts genuinely translucent cards over the same light, which is where blur reads strongest. Pick a ground and I'll animate the trip screen — scroll-under blur, sheet spring, and the total counting up as a row lands.

1Splitiful — the shipped flow, screen by screen

The app as it stands, drawn from the codebase with tokens, components and icons matched to src/app/globals.css, src/components/ui.tsx and icons.tsx. Four things define the flow. Nothing is gated up front any more: "Create your first trip" starts a trip instantly with no email and no code, and opening an invite link lands on the trip's live totals and who-owes-who with no sign-in at all. Email is asked for once, in context, at the moment it's needed — adding an expense, inviting, settling — and it is now a 6-digit code, not a magic link; for the organizer that confirmation happens inline on the trip page itself. Before it, the mutating entry points are hidden rather than disabled: no bottom bar, no roster or settle-up icons, just the card that asks. One email can hold several trips, so there is a trips list now. And the roster carries name-only placeholder members — a full share at $0 paid, shown everywhere a member appears, never a payer — while expenses carry their own date and your own rows are editable. Numbers run consistently across all seventeen screens: six members, $2,737.02 logged, $456.17 each, five transfers to clear it.
